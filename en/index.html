FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you work internationally?

Yes. Our customers are located worldwide. Tell us your delivery country and volumes in the quote request.

What products do you offer?

Three ranges, in reels and sheets, from 20 to 1,500 g/m²: graphic paper (offset, coated, LWC, newsprint…), packaging paper (kraft, kraftliner, testliner, bleached kraft, greaseproof…) and board (GC1, GC2, GD2, SBS…). We also offer custom packaging, processing machinery and technical support.

Where does your paper come from?

We work with European paper mills and select the right grade for each production.
